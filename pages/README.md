# Your pages

Your dashboard, interview flashcards, portfolio site and interview-brief template. Double-click one to open it in your browser, or ask the assistant to refresh or open it. They start as copies of `templates/blank/pages/` (with example data) the first time you open this folder, and take on your look from `profile/design.md`.

These pages are yours: they are not part of the app's download, git ignores them, and an update never changes them. If an update improves how a page works, the assistant will offer to bring the change into your copy.
