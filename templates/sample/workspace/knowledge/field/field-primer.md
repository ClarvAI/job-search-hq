## 1. How the field works
Product marketing sits between product, sales and marketing: it decides who a product is for and what it's compared with, writes the story, launches it, and arms sales to sell it.

## 2. Rules, standards and processes everyone is expected to know
- **Launch tiers:** big, medium and small launches get different amounts of work. Your hook: Insights was a top-tier launch.

## 3. Vocabulary
- **Positioning:** who it's for, what it's compared with, and why it wins.
- **Messaging:** the words that bring the positioning to life for each audience.
- **Battlecard:** a one-page guide for sales calls against one competitor.
- **Usage-based pricing:** customers pay for what they use instead of per seat.
