# How You Write

> SAMPLE: Alex Rivera's writing style (made up).
> Every cover letter, email and message should sound like you on a good day, not like a press release.

## About your voice

- Short, direct sentences; leads with the point.
- Friendly but not chatty; signs off "Thanks, Alex".
- Uses concrete numbers instead of adjectives.

## Rules

- Short sentences. Plain words. Concrete numbers and names.
- Lead with the point. In an email, the ask goes in the first two sentences.
- Specific beats impressive: "I cut report turnaround from three days to one" beats "I drove operational excellence."
- One idea per paragraph. Cover letters: 3–4 short paragraphs, under 300 words. Cold emails: under 120 words. LinkedIn connection notes: 200 characters max (free accounts); messages to existing connections: under about 600.
- Confident, not desperate. State availability as a plain fact: "Available to start two weeks after an offer."
- No em dashes. Use commas, periods or parentheses.

## Never use (instant AI tells)

delve, passionate, thrilled, excited to apply, I am writing to express, leverage (as a verb), spearheaded, synergy, dynamic, cutting-edge, innovative solutions, tapestry, testament, pivotal, unwavering, transformative, seamless, robust (outside technical specs), elevate, empower, "in today's fast-paced world," "I believe I would be a great fit," "aligns perfectly with," "drive impact," "not only X but also Y," lists of three adjectives
