# Field Primer

What interviewers in the user's field expect a candidate at their level to explain in plain words. `/interview-prep` and `/mock-interview` quiz the user from this, and `/pages` turns it into flashcards. Each topic ends with **Your hook**: where the user's own experience (from `profile/master-resume.md`) connects. Topics with no hook are gaps to study, not things to claim.

Keep it accurate and current: cite a source for anything that changes (regulations, standards, tools, market facts), and date it.

## Shape (fill per field)

### 1. How the field works
How work flows, who the customers are, what the main job families do day to day.

### 2. Rules, standards and processes everyone is expected to know
Regulations, standards, methodologies, frameworks, certifications. Only what a candidate at the user's level is expected to know.

### 3. Vocabulary
Terms interviewers use without explaining, each with a one-line plain definition.

### 4. Technical topics by target role
For each of the user's target roles: the 5–8 concepts most often tested, each with a plain explanation and **Your hook**.

### 5. Judgment questions particular to this field
Situations where the field has a "right way" (safety, compliance, ethics, client confidentiality, escalation). What good looks like.

### 6. What's happening now
3–6 current trends or news items worth mentioning in an interview, with dates and sources.
