# Story Bank (STAR)

> Interview answers come from here. Each story is **S**ituation, **T**ask, **A**ction, **R**esult, told in about 2 minutes.
> `/start` drafts the first stories from your resume. The **[ASK]** parts are the details that make a story believable: what went wrong, what you personally did, what you'd do differently. The assistant fills these in with you during `/start` or `/mock-interview`, and never invents them.
> Tag each story with the questions it can answer.

## Gaps to prepare for

Stories almost every interviewer asks for. Ask the user for one each:
- A failure or mistake and what you learned **[ASK]**
- A conflict with a coworker or manager **[ASK]**
- A time you had to learn something fast **[ASK]**
- A time you disagreed with a decision **[ASK]**
- A time you led something (formally or not) **[ASK]**
- Why this field / this kind of role? (your real reason) **[ASK]**
- Career changers and people returning to work: the honest, short story of the change or the gap **[ASK]**
