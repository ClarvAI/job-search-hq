About Acme
Acme Corp builds analytics software for operations teams at mid-size companies. We have about 900 employees and customers in 30 countries.

The role
You will own positioning, messaging and launches for Acme Analytics, our fastest-growing product line. You will work closely with product management, sales and customer success, and report to the Director of Product Marketing.

What you will do
- Own positioning and messaging for Acme Analytics and its add-ons.
- Plan and run two to three major launches a year, from research to sales training.
- Build competitive intelligence and battlecards for the sales team.
- Run customer interviews and win/loss analysis, and turn findings into messaging.
- Partner with demand generation on campaigns and the pricing page.

What you bring
- 6+ years of product marketing experience in B2B software.
- A track record of launches with measurable results.
- Experience enabling sales teams (battlecards, decks, training).
- Comfort with customer research and data; SQL is a plus.
- Experience with HubSpot or Salesforce.

Nice to have
- Experience marketing analytics or data products.
- Experience with usage-based pricing.
