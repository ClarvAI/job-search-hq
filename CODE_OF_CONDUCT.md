# Code of conduct

Job Search HQ is for people looking for work, and many of the people who open issues here are going through a stressful time. Keep that in mind in every issue, discussion and pull request.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to AI apps, to GitHub, or to English.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume someone is wrong.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect people's privacy: never post anyone's resume, email, phone number, contact list or other personal details, including your own. Use made-up examples.

## What isn't OK

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background, job history, employment status, age, disability or language.
- Sexual language or imagery, threats, or encouraging harm.
- Publishing someone else's private information, or pressuring people to share theirs.
- Spam, advertising, or using issues to recruit or sell services.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, report it privately to the maintainer (the repository owner on GitHub) rather than replying in public. Security problems go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break this code, in proportion to what happened. This applies everywhere the project is discussed: issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent the project.
