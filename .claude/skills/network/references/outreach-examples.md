# Outreach examples

Good versions of each message type. The people, companies, schools and numbers below are all fictional (the sender is "Alex Rivera"; fields vary on purpose). Use them for shape and length, not as copy-paste: every real message is written in the user's voice (`profile/voice.md`), names something specific about the person, and uses only true facts about the user. Character counts include spaces.

## LinkedIn connection note (kind: connection-note, 200 characters max)

> Hi Maria, I'm a mechanical engineer (4 years in plastics molding at Northfield Plastics) looking at process roles at Acme Devices. Open to a 15-minute chat about your team? Thanks, Alex

(185 characters.) Shared link first (school, past employer, same field), one specific ask, easy to say no to.

## Message to an existing connection (kind: message, under ~600 characters)

> Hi Tom, hope things at Brightline are going well. I'm starting a search for brand marketing roles in consumer goods. The launch campaign we ran together is still my favorite story in interviews. Would you have 15 minutes in the next couple of weeks to tell me how you'd approach the search, or who I should talk to? Thanks, Alex

## Informational-interview email (cold or alumni, under 100 words)

Shared link first, no mention of jobs, a general interest, the ask as a question, more about them than the sender.

**Subject:** Fellow Example State alum, question about product marketing at Acme

> Hi Ms. Patel,
>
> I saw you're an Example State alum working in product marketing at Acme. I've spent three years in customer success at a software company, and I'm trying to learn what the first year in a role like yours looks like before I make the move.
>
> Would you have 15 minutes for a call in the next two weeks? I'm free most afternoons after 2 pm, or whenever suits you.
>
> Thank you,
> Alex Rivera

## Former manager (the warmest path)

## Reconnecting with someone you haven't spoken to in years (experienced hire)

**Subject:** Long time, quick question

> Hi Dana,
>
> It's been a while since the Riverside warehouse project. I saw you're running quality at Harbor Foods now, which looks like a big change from Northfield.
>
> I'm starting to look at quality roles in food manufacturing. Would you have 15 minutes in the next couple of weeks to tell me how you made the move?
>
> Thanks,
> Alex

(About 60 words.) Name the shared history, notice something about them, one small ask. No apology for the gap needed.

## Message to a recruiter after applying (email or kind: message)

> Hi Jordan, I applied this week for the Process Engineer role at Acme Devices (job ID 24-1187). I've spent four years on injection-molding lines at Northfield Plastics, where I cut scrap 12% on our highest-volume part. Are you the right person to ask about this role, and what does the process look like from here? Thanks, Alex Rivera

(Under 400 characters.) Role and job ID, two lines of fit, one question. Never "what jobs do you have?"

## Thank-you after an informational call (within 24 hours)

> Hi Ms. Patel, thank you for the time yesterday. Your point about owning one metric in your first quarter is something I'll use in my interviews. If anyone else on your team would be open to a quick chat, I'd be grateful for an introduction. Thanks again, Alex

## Referral ask (1–2 weeks after a good conversation, when a matching posting exists)

**Subject:** Acme Product Marketing Manager (R-512345), would you refer me?

> Hi Ms. Patel,
>
> Acme just posted a Product Marketing Manager role on your team (R-512345, link below) that fits what you described. Would you be comfortable referring me? Here's a two-line summary you can forward:
>
> "Alex Rivera, three years in customer success at Lumen Software. Built the onboarding email series that cut first-month churn 18% and ran customer interviews for two product launches."
>
> My resume is attached. No worries at all if it's not a fit.
>
> Thank you,
> Alex

## Follow-up after no reply (one gentle bump, 5–7 business days later, then stop)

> Hi Ms. Patel, just bumping this in case it got buried. Totally understand if now's busy. Thanks, Alex

## Closing the loop (after an intro or referral helped)

> Hi Ms. Patel, quick update: I interviewed with your team last week and have a second round on Friday. Thank you again for the referral; it made a real difference. Alex

## Avoid

- "I am writing to express my interest…", "I hope this email finds you well", long life stories, attaching a resume to a first cold note, asking a stranger for a job or referral in the first message, more than one follow-up (two unanswered messages in all, then stop).
