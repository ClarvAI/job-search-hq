# Work-search requirements by jurisdiction

Checked 2026-10-08 on each agency's own website (state `.gov` sites, gov.uk, acas.org.uk). These rules change (Michigan raised its number in July 2026; Texas updated its county numbers in August 2026), and the user's own claim letter or agency account always wins over this file. Before telling the user a number, re-check the official page if you can, and quote the date you checked.

**How to read this file**
- "Verified" items were read on the official page or form. Items marked *(partly verified)* come from the official site's text as shown in search results because the site blocked automated reading; tell the user to confirm them on the page. "Check your state's site" means no official number or rule was found: don't fill the gap.
- **Fields** list what the official log or online form asks for, in its order. Use that order for the export's columns. Never put an SSN, claimant ID or claim number in any file; if the form asks for one, the user writes it by hand.
- If a state isn't listed: fetch its unemployment agency's work-search page (find it through CareerOneStop, https://www.careeronestop.org/LocalHelp/UnemploymentBenefits/unemployment-benefits.aspx) and use the default fields: date, employer, address or website, contact person, how contacted, position, result.

## California (EDD)

- **Per week:** EDD publishes no number. Claimants must "actively look for work each week" and follow the work-search instructions on the Notice of Unemployment Insurance Award (DE 429Z). Check your state's site and your own DE 429Z.
- **Counts:** searching and applying, interviews, civil service exams, talking with friends, past employers or community members about openings, sending or posting a resume, registering with staffing services, job-seeker events, preparing for the search, approved training. Union members registered with their union and meeting its dispatch rules answer "yes".
- **Registration:** CalJOBS, with an active online resume, within 21 days of the Notice of Requirement to Register for Work (DE 8405).
- **Fields:** EDD says to keep "a record of your job search dates and contacts"; no official field list. Use the default fields.
- **Reported:** certify every two weeks (UI Online, Tele-Cert or the paper DE 4581); you don't submit the record but must have it "immediately available if we ask".
- **Keep:** not stated; check your state's site.
- Sources: https://edd.ca.gov/en/unemployment/eligibility/ · https://edd.ca.gov/en/unemployment/understanding_the_continued_claim_certification_questions/ · https://edd.ca.gov/siteassets/files/pdf_pub_ctr/de1275b.pdf · https://edd.ca.gov/siteassets/files/pdf_pub_ctr/de429z.pdf

## Texas (TWC)

- **Per week:** set by county or workforce area, from 1 to 5 (for example 5 in Harris, Dallas, Travis and Bexar); TWC's letter to the claimant gives their number. Exemptions need TWC approval (temporary layoff with a definite return date, union hiring hall, approved or Trade Act training, Shared Work).
- **Counts:** WorkInTexas.com searches and contacts, Workforce Solutions services, applying in person, by mail or online, interviews, resumes uploaded to job boards, reemployment plans, job fairs, job clubs, networking events and workshops, registering with agencies.
- **Registration:** WorkInTexas.com within three business days of applying.
- **Fields (form BN900E, Work Search Activity Log):** Date of activity · Work search activity · Type of job · Employer/service/agency name · Address, city/state/zip · Phone · Person contacted · How (mail, email, fax) · Results (hired, not hiring, start date, application filed, other).
- **Reported:** request payment every two weeks; keep the log and submit it only if TWC asks (UI Submission Portal).
- **Keep:** "for your entire benefit year, or for as long as you are receiving unemployment benefits, whichever is longer."
- Sources: https://www.twc.texas.gov/programs/unemployment-benefits/work-search-requirements · https://www.twc.texas.gov/programs/unemployment-benefits/required-number-work-search-activities-county · https://www.twc.texas.gov/sites/default/files/ui/docs/work-search-log-twc.pdf

## Florida (FloridaCommerce, Reemployment Assistance)

- **Per week:** at least 5 prospective employer contacts; at least 3 in a county of 75,000 people or fewer (Florida Statutes 443.091(1)(d); the population figure is *partly verified*). A reemployment-services visit to a CareerSource center can count. The same employer at the same location can't be listed three weeks in a row unless it has since said it's hiring. Exempt by statute: union hiring hall, temporary layoff, short-time compensation, required reemployment services.
- **Registration:** Employ Florida, before requesting payments *(partly verified)*.
- **Fields** *(partly verified, from the official guide)*: date · contact name (employer, agency, website) · method (in person, online, phone) · phone, website, address or email · type of work sought · result.
- **Reported:** enter the contacts in the payment request every two weeks.
- **Keep:** not stated; check your state's site.
- Sources: https://www.leg.state.fl.us/Statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&Search_String=&URL=0400-0499%2F0443%2FSections%2F0443.091.html · https://floridajobs.org/workforce-resources/reemployment-assistance/claimants/request-benefit-payment

## New York (NYSDOL)

- **Per week:** at least 3 work search activities, statewide. Waived for: temporary layoff with a written return date (up to 8 weeks), union referral, approved training, jury duty, Shared Work, Self Employment Assistance, a firm job offer starting soon.
- **Counts:** Career Center services, applications and resumes, job-search seminars, networking meetings, job fairs, workshops, interviews, pre-hire or civil service tests, registering with agencies, unions or placement offices, and "any other reasonable activity".
- **Registration:** none required; JobZone is one way to keep the log.
- **Fields (claimant guide P857):** date of contact · organization name and address · phone, email, web link or person contacted · position title · method of contact · result. Keep confirmation emails.
- **Reported:** certify weekly; log the week's (Sunday to Saturday) activities by midnight Saturday; give a copy if asked.
- **Keep:** paper records for one year.
- Sources: https://dol.ny.gov/services/ui/wsr · https://dol.ny.gov/work-search-frequently-asked-questions · https://dol.ny.gov/ui-claimant-guide-completing-work-search-activities-p857

## Pennsylvania (Office of UC Benefits)

- **Per week:** from the 3rd week claimed: 2 job applications and 1 work search activity. An interview can replace one of them; more than 2 applications means no activity is needed. Exempt: union hiring hall, approved or Trade Act training, a PA CareerLink RESEA session that week, a written recall date, Shared-Work. Waiver request: form UC-318.
- **Activities:** job fair; searching PA CareerLink or job banks; creating or posting a resume; contacting colleagues, former co-workers or others in your field; employment agency or school placement service; civil service or pre-employment test; a PA CareerLink program.
- **Registration:** PA CareerLink within 30 days after filing.
- **Fields (form UC-304, Work Search Record, optional but the record must hold the same):** Applications/interviews: date · employer · employer contact name · employer phone/email · how you applied · result. Activities: date · type of activity · location · contact name · contact phone/email. Week runs Sunday to Saturday.
- **Reported:** claims are filed every two weeks (biweekly; `benefits_cert_covers: 2`), and the claim asks yes/no about the work search for each of the two weeks; don't send the record unless asked. Check the claimant's own filing schedule on the UC system.
- **Start:** the requirement begins with the 3rd week claimed: count weeks from `benefits_claim_start`.
- **Two field sets:** the record has two parts (applications/interviews and activities); export them as two tables.
- **Keep:** "two years from your Application for Benefits (AB) date."
- Sources: https://www.pa.gov/en/agencies/dli/resources/for-claimants-workers/benefits-information/using-the-uc-system/claimant-faqs/work-search.html · https://benefits.uc.pa.gov/admin/gsipub/htmlarea/uploads/UC304.pdf

## Illinois (IDES)

- **Per week:** no number published; claimants must be "actively looking for work on your own initiative" every week claimed. Check your state's site and any IDES letter.
- **Registration:** IllinoisJobLink.com (or at an IDES office); searches there are recorded automatically.
- **Fields (form ADJ034F, Work Search Record):** contact date · name and address of contact · person contacted · method of contact · type of work sought · results.
- **Reported:** certify every two weeks; keep the record and produce it on request.
- **Keep:** until 53 weeks after the end of each week (longer if an appeal is pending).
- Sources: https://ides.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/ides/ides_forms_and_publications/ADJ034F.pdf · https://ides.illinois.gov/unemployment/resources/unemployment-insurance-benefit-handbook/ui-benefit-handbook-work-search-and-availability.html

## Georgia (GDOL)

- **Per week:** at least 3 new, verifiable activities on 3 or more days, normally with different employers. Approved trainees don't report work search.
- **Counts:** applying for suitable jobs, interviews, reemployment services at a workforce center, a work search plan, creating or uploading a resume. Not: applying for jobs you're unqualified for, viewing listings without applying.
- **Registration:** WorkSource Georgia (WorkSourceGAPortal.com) within 10 days of the first payment (older pages say EmployGeorgia).
- **Fields (form DOL-2798, Weekly Work Search Activities Record):** activity · method (internet, in person, email, telephone) · new or follow-up · date · job title or requisition number · business name · business address or website · phone · person contacted or email · outcome. Online applications also need the job site, job ID or listing URL.
- **Reported:** weekly: request payment, then submit the work search record (MyUI) the same week. Weeks run Sunday to Saturday.
- **Keep:** "Retain a copy" (no period stated); check your state's site.
- Sources: https://dol.georgia.gov/learn-about-work-search-requirements · https://dol.georgia.gov/document/unemployment-benefits/ui-weekly-work-search-record-dol-2798/download

## Ohio (ODJFS)

- **Per week:** 2 work search activities (policy effective 2025-09-01). Waived for approved training or a confirmed temporary layoff (return within 45 days); met by school attendance or union hiring hall.
- **Counts:** applications or resumes, assigned reemployment activities, a reemployment plan or searchable resume, the OhioMeansJobs Career Profile, job fairs, training, a professional networking profile, interviews, any other good-faith strategy. Not: calling or dropping by to ask if a business is hiring.
- **Registration:** OhioMeansJobs.com (automatic); searchable resume by week 4, Career Profile by week 8.
- **Fields:** no official list ("documented and verifiable"); use the default fields.
- **Reported:** on each weekly claim.
- **Keep:** 18 months.
- Sources: https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/jfs.ohio.gov/ouio/UIPolicy/ActiveSearchForWork.pdf · https://unemployment.ohio.gov/cmt/cmtHighlights.html

## North Carolina (DES)

- **Per week:** at least 3 job contacts from the week you file; one may be a reemployment activity.
- **Counts:** applications, resumes or letters of interest; meeting or interviewing with an employer (including job fairs and video); emailing an employer; reemployment activities (training, career counseling, assessments, job clubs, career fairs); first NCWorks.gov registration (once).
- **Registration:** NCWorks.gov.
- **Fields:** date · company or activity name · contact method (in person, phone, email, online) · matching contact information (address, phone, email or website). Paper form NCCLM 506e.
- **Reported:** enter contacts in your account before each weekly certification.
- **Keep:** DES recommends 2 years; the form may say 5 *(partly verified)*; keeping 5 is safe.
- Sources: https://www.des.nc.gov/worksearch · https://www.des.nc.gov/individuals/weekly-requirements

## Michigan (UIA) *(partly verified: the site blocked automated reading)*

- **Per week:** at least 3 unique activities each week certified, from 2026-07-19 (it was 1). Exceptions include a temporary layoff waiver (recall within 45 days, requested by the employer) and Work Share. Not valid: the same job at the same employer within 4 weeks, only asking whether a job is open, browsing job boards.
- **Counts:** applications in person or online, job fairs, a profile or resume on MiTalent.org or similar, job-search workshops, job shadowing, internships.
- **Registration:** Pure Michigan Talent Connect (MiTalent.org).
- **Fields (form UIA 6507, Work Search Activity Worksheet):** date · activity · employer, organization or search engine · address or online location. Confirm the full list on the form.
- **Reported:** in MiWAM at each biweekly certification.
- **Keep:** confirmations and documentation for up to 2 years.
- Sources: https://www.michigan.gov/leo/bureaus-agencies/uia/uia-resources-for-claimants/finding-employment-work-search/work-search-requirement · https://www.michigan.gov/leo/-/media/Project/Websites/leo/Documents/UIA/Claimant-Forms/UIA-6507.pdf

## New Jersey (NJDOL)

- **Per week:** the current work search page and log say a minimum of 3 different employer contacts each week is a reasonable search (older handbook pages say 1; use 3). Exceptions: check your state's site.
- **Counts:** phone, internet and in-person contacts, and sending resumes; union contacts. Print a copy of each internet application and the response.
- **Fields (form BC-514, Work Search Log):** date of contact · employer name, address and phone or internet address · method (in person, phone, internet, resume) · person or website contacted · position applied for · application taken? · result. Weeks run Sunday to Saturday.
- **Reported:** certify weekly online (or every 2 weeks by phone); contacts can be requested any time, with random audits.
- **Keep:** not stated; check your state's site.
- Sources: https://www.nj.gov/labor/myunemployment/before/about/howtoapply/worksearch.shtml · https://www.nj.gov/labor/myunemployment/assets/pdfs/BC_514.pdf

## Washington (ESD)

- **Per week:** 3 job search activities (employer contacts, WorkSource activities or other approved activities); the exact same activity can't count twice. Survivors of domestic violence or stalking: 1. Waivers include approved partial unemployment, standby during a temporary layoff, SharedWork, approved training and some apprentices; full-referral union members search through the union.
- **Counts:** employer contacts (email, phone, mail, in person), WorkSource activities and workshops, job fairs, registering on job sites, approved courses and videos.
- **Registration:** WorkSourceWA.com within 1 week of the benefit-rights notice (profile visible to employers).
- **Fields (Job-search log, EMS 10313):** contact date · kind (employer contact, WorkSource activity, other). Employer contact: job title or reference number · employer name · how (in person, online, phone, email, mail) · type (application/resume, interview, inquiry) · employer address, website or email, phone. WorkSource or other activity: what you did · documentation · where.
- **Reported:** entered in each weekly claim (eServices); send the log only if asked.
- **Keep:** at least 30 days after the end of the benefit year or after benefits stop, whichever is later.
- Sources: https://esd.wa.gov/get-financial-help/unemployment-benefits/weekly-unemployment-claims/job-search-requirements · https://esd.wa.gov/job-search-log

## Massachusetts (DUA) *(partly verified: the site blocked automated reading)*

- **Per week:** at least 3 work search activities; without them, no benefits that week. Some decisions mention 3 different days; confirm on the page. Main exception: DUA-approved training.
- **Counts:** applications, resumes or letters of interest, in-person employer visits, job fairs, networking events, workshops (private or MassHire), interviews, private employment agencies.
- **Registration:** MassHire JobQuest if selected for RESEA.
- **Fields (Work Search Activity Log, Form 1750, for phone filers):** date · position · pay rate · employer name and address · job ID or person contacted · contact email, website or phone · result. Online filers enter activities in their account.
- **Reported:** in each weekly benefit request.
- **Keep:** documents for 1 year after you stop requesting benefits.
- Sources: https://www.mass.gov/info-details/weekly-requirements-to-get-unemployment-assistance · https://www.mass.gov/doc/work-search-log-0/download

## UK: Universal Credit

- **What's expected:** the Claimant Commitment sets what the user agreed to do; it's "a record of the responsibilities that you have accepted ... and the consequences of not meeting them". People in the all work-related requirements group are usually expected to look or prepare for work for **35 hours a week**, reduced for things like caring for young children, health conditions or caring responsibilities. There's no published minimum number of applications; the work coach decides and can ask for evidence.
- **Recording:** the online account's journal ("tell us what you've done to look for work") and to-dos. Export the week as a list the user can paste into journal entries (one line per activity: date, what, employer, result) plus the hours spent, if they track them. The work coach may ask for evidence (application confirmations).
- **Sanctions:** missing what the Claimant Commitment requires can reduce payments; levels and lengths are on gov.uk. Say only that it's worth agreeing realistic tasks and telling the work coach early if something can't be done.
- **Keep:** not stated; keep the log while claiming and a while after.
- Sources: https://www.gov.uk/universal-credit/your-claimant-commitment · https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/universal-credit-and-your-claimant-commitment-quick-guide/universal-credit-and-your-claimant-commitment · https://www.gov.uk/guidance/universal-credit-sanctions

## UK: New Style Jobseeker's Allowance

- Work coach appointments every 1-2 weeks, where "you must show your work coach what you've been doing to look for work, for example proof of job applications and interviews." The printable weekly page is made for this.
- Sources: https://www.gov.uk/jobseekers-allowance/your-jsa-claim

## Elsewhere

Programs differ a lot (Canada's Employment Insurance, Australia's mutual obligations, Ireland's Jobseeker's payments, and so on). Fetch the government page for the user's program, use its fields if it lists them, and otherwise the default fields. Say "check <official page>" for anything not stated.
