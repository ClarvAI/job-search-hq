## Bullet patterns that work

- **Action + what + result:** "Cut weekly reporting time 85% by building an automated spreadsheet pipeline used by 14 sites."
- **Lead with the result when it's the headline:** "Saved $23K a year in supply costs by ..."
- **Scope words count:** how many people, sites, customers, dollars, units, users.
- No numbers? Use scale, frequency or who relied on it ("used daily by the 6-person support team").
- Never invent a number. If a good bullet lacks one, ask the user.

## Words that read as filler to recruiters

responsible for, duties included, helped with (say what you did), team player, hard-working, detail-oriented (show it instead), results-driven
