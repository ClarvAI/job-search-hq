# Disclaimer

Job Search HQ is a set of instructions and small scripts that help an AI assistant (Claude Code or Codex) help you with a job search. Please read this before you rely on it.

## Not professional advice

Nothing it produces is legal, financial, tax, immigration, or medical advice. That includes what it says about offers and negotiation, pay data, contracts and non-competes, visas and work authorization, benefits, taxes, unemployment rules, and workplace accommodations. For decisions that matter, check with a qualified professional or the official source (an employment lawyer, an immigration attorney or your school's international office, a tax adviser, your government's official sites).

## AI output can be wrong

The assistant can misread a posting, get a date or a number wrong, misremember a rule, or state something with more confidence than it deserves. Pay figures, deadlines and company facts can be out of date. Counts it works out for you (visa unemployment days left, which benefit weeks a certification covers, dates read from a spreadsheet) are estimates: the official counter, your agency or school, and your own records win. The workspace has checks (a fact check against your own resume, a voice check, reminders to verify), but they reduce mistakes; they don't remove them. Read everything before you use it.

## You are responsible for what you send and submit

The assistant drafts; you decide. You are responsible for every application, resume, cover letter, message and form answer that leaves your computer, and for checking that it's true. By default it never sends email, submits applications or posts anything for you. If you turn on a mode that lets it fill in or send something after you confirm, you are still the one approving it.

## Follow each site's rules

Job boards, social networks and employer sites have their own terms of use. Follow them. This project never automates LinkedIn and only reads public job postings, but you are responsible for how you use any site, including LinkedIn, Indeed, Handshake and employer application systems.

## Your data

- Your files stay in this folder on your computer. This project has no server, no account, and no analytics or telemetry. It doesn't phone home.
- What you work on is sent to the AI provider you use (Anthropic for Claude, OpenAI for Codex) so the assistant can respond, under that provider's terms and privacy policy and your plan's data settings.
- Connectors you choose to turn on (for example Gmail or Google Calendar) give the assistant access to that account, under the connector provider's terms. The workspace limits what it does with them (read job mail, make drafts, add your own calendar events), but the connection itself is between you and that service.
- Pages you publish (for example a portfolio link) are stored by the service that hosts them. Pages you open in a browser load fonts and libraries from public CDNs, which see your IP address.
- If this folder sits in a cloud-synced folder (Dropbox, OneDrive, iCloud, Google Drive), that service has a copy too.
