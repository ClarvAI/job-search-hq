- SKILL.md bodies touched (about 7 KB in total across nine skills): find-jobs (kind of search, scout brief, search-firm pointer), linkedin (Share profile updates), network (search firms, discretion, postdoc emails), interview-prep (scheduling around work, references), offer (hourly/contract/academic offers, notice and resignation), evaluate-job (hourly, contract and academic checks), add-job (`employment` values, `schedule:` header line, kind-of-job capture), tailor-resume (hourly, contract, academic CV formats; no page target for an academic CV), cover-letter (hourly and academic letters), sync-inbox (checkpoint, re-runs, other mailboxes), follow-up and import-linkedin (one writer at a time; colleagues at the current employer flagged "not now"). ## Decisions

- **No tracker schema change.** Gigs use existing columns: rate with its unit in `salary_range`, the arrangement first in `notes` (after any `Also:` token), the agency in `source` and `contact`, a "Decide next step" about four weeks before a contract ends.
- **`posting.md` header:** `employment:` now lists temporary, seasonal, contract-to-hire and freelance; a new optional `schedule:` line. The Apply Kit's header parser ignores unknown keys (checked `frontmatter()` in its register.tsx).
- **Availability for hourly work** is saved as plain lines under "What you want" in the user's preferences, with their yes (see Needs: B could add them to the blank file and `start`).
- **sync-inbox checkpoint:** stays the `Inbox synced through` line in `history/activity.md` (only this skill reads it; checked with grep), now with an optional `(Gmail)` / `(Outlook)` suffix; lines without one count as Gmail. Robustness: read either app's newest line; 2-day overlap also covers search date time-zone shifts; a gap over 60 days asks whether to read it all or the last 30 days; search all mail, not just the inbox, and don't depend on a label existing; a stopped run writes no checkpoint. Re-runs are idempotent by comparing with current state (oldest first, never backwards, no double `followups`/`touches`), not by storing message IDs. Pasted or saved emails never write a checkpoint.
- **sync-inbox description left unchanged** (still says Gmail) so the Codex wrapper stays in parity; the body routes other mailboxes. Proposed new description is in Needs.

## What research found (key facts and sources)

- LinkedIn Open to Work "Recruiters only" can't guarantee privacy (Help 67405); the setting that announces profile changes is "Share profile updates" under Settings & Privacy → Visibility (Help 86236), both checked 2026-10-13.
- UK statutory minimum resignation notice is one week after one month's service (Acas, updated 2025-11-24); US at-will with two weeks customary (SHRM, 2018; no usa.gov or DOL page found).
- US federal minimum wage $7.25 and overtime over 40 hours (DOL, updated 2026-07-01); ACA full-time = 30 hours a week / 130 a month (IRS); fair workweek laws in Oregon (BOLI, fetched) and NYC, Seattle, Chicago, Emeryville (city pages via search). UK part-time and zero-hours rights (GOV.UK); guaranteed hours and shift notice planned for 2027 (GOV.UK ERA timeline).
- IRS contractor tests (reviewed 2026-05-19); self-employment tax 12.4% + 2.9% (Topic 554, reviewed 2026-09-24); DOL proposed a new contractor rule on 2026-02-26, comments closed 2026-04-28, no final rule, 2024 rule not applied in investigations (DOL page fetched). IR35: client decides for medium/large clients, SDS, CEST, since 6 April 2021 (GOV.UK updated 2026-02-26, fetched). UK work-seeker fee ban with exceptions (2003 Regulations reg. 26, fetched; exceptions include company work-seekers). FTC: honest placement firms don't charge candidates.
- Claude Microsoft 365 connector: all plans including Free, work or school accounts only (no outlook.com/hotmail), Entra Global Admin consent, read-only by default with write tools opt-in (support.claude.com article 12542951, fetched 2026-10-13, "Updated this week"). Claude Code gets claude.ai connectors with a subscription login (code.claude.com MCP docs). ChatGPT has an Outlook Email app for work accounts (OpenAI Help 12512241, via search). Microsoft 365 tenants can block external auto-forwarding (Microsoft Learn, 2026-08-18). `.eml` parses with the stdlib `email` package; `.msg` (MS-OXMSG) does not.
- Academic: Berkeley CV order; UCLA/UChicago letter and statement lengths; MIT (May 2024) and UC (20 March 2025) dropped diversity statement requirements; ASHA tracker lists 11 states with restrictions (no last-updated date); NIH NRSA postdoc stipends $63,480-$77,076 for awards from 2025-10-01 (NOT-OD-26-044, fetched); AHA reported tenure-line history ads down 20% to 213 (May 2026, via search).
- USAJOBS two-page limit since 27 September 2025: no 2026 change found (OPM page title confirmed via search; the page itself returned unreadable data). Existing text in `tailoring-and-formats.md` §6 left as it is.
