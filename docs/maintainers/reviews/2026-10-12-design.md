# Design and accessibility review of the pages and documents (2026-10-12)

HANDOFF §5 items 4 (design review with real eyes) and 5 (WCAG 2.2 AA, the pages and PDFs part). Reviewer: the
"pages-a11y" maintainer agent, against the `page-review` checklist and `page-design` rules. Sample data only (the Alex
Rivera persona and the preview's sample jobs). Panels and the conversation are covered in `2026-10-12-a11y.md`.

## Method

- **Harness (new, in the repo):** `scripts/ci/visual/` (`run.sh`, `visual.mjs`, `fixtures/`, `README.md`). It copies the
  product files to a temp workspace, renders every page with `apply-theme.mjs --preview --layouts` there (never the
  repo's files), and drives the system Chrome 155 through `playwright-core` 1.56.1 with `axe-core` 4.11.0, both
  installed into a cache folder outside the repo. Documents are printed with the workspace copy of `make-pdf.sh`.
- **Real eyes:** I opened and judged the screenshots (full page, every width, light/dark, forced colors, 200% zoom,
  text spacing, print previews of page 1, every document's PDF) as a brand-new user would, not only the automated
  results. Crops and montages were made with Pillow in the scratchpad.
- **Runs:** run 1 (baseline, before fixes), run 2 (after most fixes), run 3 (final). Screenshot paths below are in the
  session scratchpad: `<scratch>/vis/run<N>/shots/<theme>/<page>-<layout>[-state]/<scheme>-<width>.png`, with
  `<scratch>` = `<scratchpad>`.
  They are not committed (no screenshots in the repo); rerun `bash scripts/ci/visual/run.sh` to regenerate them.

## Results (final run)

Run 1 (baseline): 124 failures, in five root causes (#1, #3, #4, #5 below, plus #16, a harness artifact). Run 3
(after the fixes): **0 failures** across 70 page variants and 66 documents (`<scratch>/vis/run3/summary.md`).

- axe-core, WCAG 2.0/2.1/2.2 A and AA tags: 0 violations in 560 renders (70 variants × 2 schemes × 4 widths). axe
  left 1,388 color-contrast nodes "incomplete" (text over the accent gradient, overlapping layers); those pairs are
  measured by `apply-theme.mjs` instead (every text pair on both gradient stops ≥ 4.5:1).
- Reflow: no horizontal page scroll at 320 px (= 400% zoom), 390, 768, 1440 or 640 px (200% zoom), in any variant;
  boards and the brief's phone nav scroll inside their own box. Text spacing (1.4.12): no scroll, no clipped text.
- Target size (2.5.8): no target under 24 px without the spacing or inline-link exception (axe `target-size` agrees).
- Keyboard: 280 Tab walks (1,680 stops): every stop has a visible ring of ≥ 3:1 against what is behind it, none is
  hidden behind sticky content or left off screen, no positive tabindex; Tab order follows reading order (run 3 found
  one exception, #22, fixed and re-run: 0 upward jumps). Keyboard flows:
  60/60 checks passed (flashcards Space flips and focuses the flip button, 1–4 rate and deal, focus is kept, arrows
  move, a number typed outside the practice area does nothing; brief pitch practice shows Stop, announces itself,
  Stop ends it).
- Reduced motion: no running animation and nothing left faded on any page. At rest with motion on: no text below 60%
  opacity (the portfolio's 40% sections are gone).
- Forced colors: focus visible on every stop; controls, cards, selected states and chart bars visible (screenshots
  `forced-1440.png`, `forced-390.png`).
- Print (Letter and A4, light and dark device theme): always light colors (text ≥ 4.5:1 on white). Brief `sheet` 1
  page, `detailed` 2, dashboard 1, flashcards 1, portfolio 2, in every theme.
- Documents: 66/66 one page, right paper, `parse: ok`, tagged, `Lang en`, H1 plus section H2s in reading order.

## Tagged PDFs and reading order

Chrome 155 headless `--print-to-pdf` already writes **tagged PDFs** (`pdfinfo` "Tagged: yes", `/MarkInfo /Marked true`,
`/StructTreeRoot`, `/Lang (en)`, `/DisplayDocTitle true`), with H1, H2, H3, P, L/LI, Link and Strong in DOM order.
All 66 document PDFs were tagged with `Lang en`; the tag-tree heading order matched the visible order (designed
resume after the fix: H1, H2 Experience, H2 Projects, H2 Education, H2 Skills, H3, H3, H2 Certifications), and
`make-pdf`'s `pdftotext` check read name, contact, headings and dates in order (`parse: ok`) in all 66.
`--generate-pdf-document-outline` adds bookmarks from the headings; for one-page documents they add little, so
`make-pdf` doesn't pass it (optional; the flag would live in `pdf-common.sh`). Screen-reader reading of the PDFs
(VoiceOver, NVDA) was **not verified**.
